Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains how [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] ("we", "us") handles personal data in the InstaRider and InstaDriver apps and the related website and admin tools in [TOWN, STATE, INDIA]. We aim to follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Who is responsible

[YOUR BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS]. Contact and grievance officer: [NAME], [EMAIL], [PHONE]. We will respond to privacy requests within [NUMBER] days.

2. What we collect and why

All users: name, mobile number (used to sign you in with a one-time code; we do not use passwords), the trips you take or drive, and your device location while you use the app so we can match rides, show maps and calculate fares.

InstaRiders: places you save, an emergency contact you choose (name and number), and ratings you give.

InstaDrivers: photographs of your vehicle, number plate, registration certificate (RC), driving licence, insurance and a selfie, your vehicle details, and your location while you are online. We use these only to check that you and your vehicle are eligible to carry passengers, and to keep the platform safe.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see it

  • The InstaDriver sees your name and the pickup and drop-off points for a ride you book. The InstaRider sees the driver's name, rating, vehicle type and number.
  • Phone numbers are not shown to the other person in the app.
  • Our authorised administrators can see accounts, trips and driver documents to run the service, handle complaints and respond to emergencies.
  • If you share a trip, anyone with the link can see that trip's status, route and the driver's live position until the trip ends plus one hour.
  • We may disclose data to authorities where the law requires it.
  • Service providers we use: [HOSTING PROVIDER], [MAPS/ADDRESS SEARCH PROVIDER], [SMS PROVIDER IF ANY].

4. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is active. Trip records (including the pickup and drop-off points) are kept for [NUMBER] years for fee, safety and dispute purposes; when you delete your account we remove your name from them. Driver documents are deleted when the driver account is deleted. We store only your most recent location and replace it as you move; we do not keep a location history.

5. Your rights

You can see and correct your name in the app (Profile), delete your account in the app (Settings, Delete account; see also /legal/delete-account), and contact us to access, correct or erase your data or to withdraw consent. There is no password: you sign in with a one-time code sent to your mobile number, so keep your SIM and phone safe. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Security

One-time codes are stored only in scrambled form and expire after minutes, connections use encryption in transit, driver documents are stored privately and only our administrators can open them. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you suspect misuse.

7. Children

The apps are for people aged 18 and over.

8. Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
